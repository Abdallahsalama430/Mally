# Maly
# About The Project
Managing bills and subscriptions is difficult when expenses come from many sources. This app gathers all your subscriptions and monthly spending in one simple place. It connects directly to your bank account to trace payments and alerts you if you pass your set budget, keeping money tracking completely effortless.
